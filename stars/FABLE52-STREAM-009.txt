'''Albert Einstein''' (14 March 1879 - 18 April 1955) was a German-born theoretical
physicist. He developed the [[theory of relativity]], one of the two pillars of modern
[[physics]]. He received the 1921 [[Nobel Prize in Physics]] "for his services to
theoretical physics".

== Early life ==
Einstein was born in [[Ulm]], in the Kingdom of Wurttemberg, on 14 March 1879. In 1880 the
family moved to [[Munich]]. He attended the Luitpold Gymnasium from 1888 to 1894.
