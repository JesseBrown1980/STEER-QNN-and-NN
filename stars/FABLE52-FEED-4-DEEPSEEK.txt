      <text xml:space="preserve">In [[mathematics]] and [[computer science]], an '''algorithm''' is a
finite sequence of instructions for solving a problem. The word comes from the name of the
9th-century Persian mathematician [[Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi|al-Khwarizmi]].

== History ==
The concept was formalised in 1936 through [[Alan Turing]]'s [[Turing machine]]s and
[[Alonzo Church]]'s [[lambda calculus]].

== Examples ==
* [[Euclidean algorithm]] &amp;mdash; the greatest common divisor of two numbers
* [[Sieve of Eratosthenes]] &amp;mdash; all primes up to a limit
* [[Binary search]] &amp;mdash; a sorted list, in O(log ''n'') time
* [[Quicksort]], [[mergesort]], [[heapsort]]
