'''Amsterdam''' is the [[capital]] of the [[Netherlands]]. It lies on the banks of the
[[IJ (bay)|IJ]] bay and the [[Amstel]] river. The city was founded in the late [[12th
century]] as a small fishing village. It is known for its [[canal]]s, the
[[Rijksmuseum]], the [[Van Gogh Museum]] and the [[Anne Frank House]].

== History ==
{{main|History of Amsterdam}}
The earliest recorded use of the name is from a certificate dated [[October 27]],
[[1275]]. Amsterdam was granted [[city rights]] in [[1300]] or [[1306]]. In the [[17th
century]], the [[Dutch Golden Age]], it became one of the wealthiest cities in the world.

== Transport ==
[[Image:Amsterdam tram.jpg|thumb|right|250px|A tram in the city centre]]
The city has 16 [[tram]] lines and 4 [[rapid transit|metro]] lines.
[[Amsterdam Schiphol Airport]] is less than 20 minutes by train from the central station.
