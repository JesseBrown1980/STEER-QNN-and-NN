'''France''' (French: ''République française''), is a country whose metropolitan territory is
located in [[Western Europe]]. It is bordered by [[Belgium]], [[Luxembourg]], [[Germany]],
[[Switzerland]], [[Italy]], [[Monaco]], [[Andorra]] and [[Spain]]. Its capital and largest
city is [[Paris]]. France is a founding member of the [[European Union]] and of the [[United
Nations]], and is one of the five permanent members of the [[United Nations Security
Council]]. The French Republic is a unitary semi-presidential republic.

== History ==
The borders of modern France are roughly the same as those of ancient [[Gaul]], which was
inhabited by Celtic ''Gauls''. Gaul was conquered for Rome by [[Julius Caesar]] in the 1st
century BC. In the 4th century AD, Gaul's eastern frontier along the [[Rhine]] was overrun
by Germanic tribes, principally the [[Franks]], from whom the ancient name of "Francie" was
derived. The [[Treaty of Verdun]] (843) divided [[Charlemagne]]'s empire into three parts.
The monarchy reached its height during the 17th century and the reign of [[Louis XIV of
France|Louis XIV]]. The [[French Revolution]] of 1789 overthrew the monarchy; [[Napoleon
Bonaparte]] seized control of the Republic in 1799 and made himself Emperor in 1804.

'''India''', officially the '''Republic of India''', is a country in [[South Asia]]. It is
the seventh-largest country by geographical area, the second most populous country, and the
most populous [[democracy]] in the world. Bounded by the [[Indian Ocean]] on the south, the
[[Arabian Sea]] on the west, and the [[Bay of Bengal]] on the east, India has a coastline of
7,517 kilometres. It borders [[Pakistan]] to the west; [[China]], [[Nepal]], and [[Bhutan]]
to the north-east; and [[Bangladesh]] and [[Burma]] to the east. Its capital is [[New
Delhi]]. Home to the [[Indus Valley Civilization]], it became an independent nation in 1947
after a struggle for independence that was marked by [[Mahatma Gandhi|non-violent]] resistance.

'''Isaac Newton''' (4 January 1643 – 31 March 1727) was an English [[physicist]],
[[mathematician]], [[astronomer]], [[natural philosopher]], and [[alchemist]]. His
''[[Philosophiæ Naturalis Principia Mathematica]]'', published in 1687, described
[[universal gravitation]] and the three [[Newton's laws of motion|laws of motion]], laying
the groundwork for [[classical mechanics]]. He shares credit with [[Gottfried Leibniz]] for
the development of the [[calculus]]. Newton was born at [[Woolsthorpe Manor]] in
[[Lincolnshire]]. He was educated at [[Trinity College, Cambridge]], where he became
[[Lucasian Professor of Mathematics]] in 1669. He was knighted by [[Anne of Great
Britain|Queen Anne]] in 1705 and is buried in [[Westminster Abbey]].

'''Islam''' (Arabic: الإسلام) is a [[monotheism|monotheistic]] religion based upon the
[[Qur'an]]. '''Christianity''' is a monotheistic religion centred on the life and teachings
of [[Jesus]] as presented in the [[New Testament]]. '''Buddhism''' is a religion and
philosophy based on the teachings of [[Gautama Buddha]], who lived in the 5th century BC.
'''Hinduism''' is a religious tradition that originated in the [[Indian subcontinent]].
'''Judaism''' is the religion of the [[Jewish people]], based on the [[Torah]].

'''Computer''': a computer is a machine that manipulates data according to a list of
instructions. The first devices that resemble modern computers date to the mid-20th century
(around 1940–1945). Early electronic computers were the size of a large room, consuming as
much power as several hundred modern personal computers. The '''[[Internet]]''' is a
worldwide, publicly accessible series of interconnected computer networks that transmit
data by [[packet switching]] using the standard [[Internet Protocol]] (IP). The '''[[World
Wide Web]]''' was invented in 1989 by [[Tim Berners-Lee]] at [[CERN]].

'''Julius Caesar''' (13 July 100 BC – 15 March 44 BC) was a Roman military and political
leader. He played a critical role in the transformation of the [[Roman Republic]] into the
[[Roman Empire]]. '''Cleopatra VII''' (69 BC – 30 BC) was the last pharaoh of [[Ancient
Egypt]]. '''Genghis Khan''' (c. 1162 – 1227) was the founder of the [[Mongol Empire]].
'''Joan of Arc''' (c. 1412 – 30 May 1431) is a national heroine of France and a saint of
the [[Roman Catholic Church]]. '''Elizabeth I''' (7 September 1533 – 24 March 1603) was
Queen of England from 17 November 1558 until her death. '''George Washington''' (February
22, 1732 – December 14, 1799) was the first [[President of the United States]] (1789–1797).
'''Karl Marx''' (5 May 1818 – 14 March 1883) was a German philosopher and political
economist. '''Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi''' (2 October 1869 – 30 January 1948) was a major
political and spiritual leader of India. '''Winston Churchill''' (30 November 1874 – 24
January 1965) was [[Prime Minister of the United Kingdom]] from 1940 to 1945 and again from
1951 to 1955. '''Nelson Mandela''' (born 18 July 1918) was the first [[President of South
Africa]] to be elected in fully representative democratic elections.

The '''Nile''' is a major north-flowing river in [[Africa]], generally regarded as the
longest river in the world at 6,650 km. The '''Sahara''' is the world's largest hot desert,
at over 9,000,000 km². The '''Pacific Ocean''' is the largest of the Earth's oceanic
divisions, covering 169.2 million km². '''Antarctica''' is Earth's southernmost continent;
about 98% of it is covered by ice. The '''Himalaya''' is a mountain range in Asia,
separating the Indian subcontinent from the [[Tibetan Plateau]]. The '''Great Wall of
China''' is a series of stone and earthen fortifications built between the 5th century BC
and the 16th century. The '''Eiffel Tower''' is an iron tower built on the [[Champ de
Mars]] beside the [[Seine]] in Paris; it is 324 m high and was completed in 1889.

'''Photosynthesis''' is the conversion of light energy into chemical energy by living
organisms. The raw materials are [[carbon dioxide]] and [[water]]; the energy source is
[[sunlight]]; and the end-products are [[oxygen]] and (energy rich) [[carbohydrates]].

'''Evolution''' is the change in the inherited traits of a population from one generation
to the next. [[Natural selection]] was proposed by [[Charles Darwin]] in ''[[On the Origin
of Species]]'' (1859). The '''cell''' is the structural and functional unit of all known
living organisms. The '''heart''' is a muscular organ responsible for pumping [[blood]]
through the [[blood vessel]]s. The human body has 206 [[bone]]s.

'''Jazz''' is a musical art form which originated around the beginning of the 20th century
in [[New Orleans]]. '''Opera''' is an art form in which singers and musicians perform a
dramatic work combining text ([[libretto]]) and musical score. '''The Lord of the Rings'''
is an epic [[high fantasy]] novel written by [[J. R. R. Tolkien]], published in three
volumes in 1954 and 1955. '''Don Quixote''' was written by [[Miguel de Cervantes]] and
published in two volumes in 1605 and 1615. '''Mona Lisa''' is a 16th-century oil painting
by [[Leonardo da Vinci]], in the [[Louvre]].
