'''Spain''' (Spanish: ''España'') is a country in southwestern [[Europe]] on the [[Iberian
Peninsula]]. Its capital is [[Madrid]]. '''Italy''' is a country in southern Europe; its
capital is [[Rome]]. '''Greece''' is in southeastern Europe; its capital is [[Athens]].
'''Egypt''' is a country in [[North Africa]]; the [[Nile]] flows through it and its capital
is [[Cairo]]. '''Kenya''' is a country in [[East Africa]]; its capital is [[Nairobi]].

The '''First World War''' (1914–1918) was fought mainly in Europe. More than nine million
soldiers died. It ended with the [[Armistice with Germany|armistice]] of 11 November 1918
and the [[Treaty of Versailles]] of 1919. The [[League of Nations]] was founded in 1920.
The '''Great Depression''' began with the [[Wall Street Crash of 1929]]. The '''United
Nations''' was founded in 1945 and has its headquarters in [[New York City]].

'''Leonardo da Vinci''' (15 April 1452 – 2 May 1519) was an Italian painter, sculptor,
architect, engineer and scientist. '''Michelangelo''' (6 March 1475 – 18 February 1564)
painted the ceiling of the [[Sistine Chapel]]. '''Rembrandt''' (15 July 1606 – 4 October
1669) was a Dutch painter. '''Vincent van Gogh''' (30 March 1853 – 29 July 1890) painted
''[[The Starry Night]]'' in 1889. '''Pablo Picasso''' (25 October 1881 – 8 April 1973) was
a Spanish painter and sculptor, co-founder of [[Cubism]].

The '''telephone''' was patented by [[Alexander Graham Bell]] in 1876. The '''light
bulb''' was developed by [[Thomas Edison]] in 1879. The '''aeroplane''' was first flown by
the [[Wright brothers]] on 17 December 1903. '''Penicillin''' was discovered by [[Alexander
Fleming]] in 1928. The '''transistor''' was invented at [[Bell Labs]] in 1947. The first
artificial satellite, ''[[Sputnik 1]]'', was launched by the [[Soviet Union]] on 4 October
1957. [[Yuri Gagarin]] became the first human in space on 12 April 1961.

The '''heart''' pumps blood; the '''lungs''' exchange oxygen and carbon dioxide; the
'''liver''' filters the blood; the '''kidneys''' remove waste; the '''brain''' is the
centre of the nervous system and contains about 86 billion neurons. An adult human has
32 teeth. Normal body temperature is about 37 °C.
