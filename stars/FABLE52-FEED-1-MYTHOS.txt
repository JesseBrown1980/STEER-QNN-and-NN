The '''United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland''' is a country in [[Western
Europe]]. It comprises [[England]], [[Scotland]], [[Wales]] and [[Northern Ireland]]. Its
capital is [[London]]. The '''River Thames''' flows through southern England. The
[[Parliament of the United Kingdom]] meets in the [[Palace of Westminster]]. The country was
formed by the [[Acts of Union 1707]], which united the kingdoms of England and Scotland.

In the 19th century the [[British Empire]] became the largest empire in history. The
[[Industrial Revolution]] began in Britain in the 18th century, with the invention of the
[[steam engine]] by [[James Watt]] and the growth of the [[textile]] industry in
[[Lancashire]]. By 1850 more than half of the population lived in towns and cities.

'''Charles Dickens''' (7 February 1812 – 9 June 1870) was an English novelist of the
[[Victorian era]]. His works include ''[[Oliver Twist]]'' (1838), ''[[A Christmas Carol]]''
(1843), ''[[David Copperfield (novel)|David Copperfield]]'' (1850), ''[[Bleak House]]''
(1853), ''[[A Tale of Two Cities]]'' (1859) and ''[[Great Expectations]]'' (1861). He was
born in [[Portsmouth]] and died at [[Gads Hill Place]] in [[Kent]]. He is buried in the
[[Poets' Corner]] of [[Westminster Abbey]].

The '''piano''' is a musical instrument played by means of a keyboard. It was invented by
[[Bartolomeo Cristofori]] in [[Florence]] around the year 1700. A modern piano has 88 keys,
52 white and 36 black. The '''violin''' has four strings tuned in perfect fifths. The
'''guitar''' usually has six strings. The '''symphony''' is an extended musical
composition, usually for [[orchestra]]. [[Ludwig van Beethoven]] wrote nine; [[Joseph
Haydn]] wrote 104; [[Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart]] wrote 41.

A '''triangle''' is a polygon with three sides and three angles. The sum of the angles is
180 degrees. A '''circle''' is the set of all points in a plane at a given distance from a
centre; its circumference is 2π''r'' and its area is π''r''². A '''square''' has four equal
sides. A '''cube''' has six faces, eight vertices and twelve edges; a cube of side three
contains twenty-seven unit cubes, and the one in the middle touches none of the faces.
